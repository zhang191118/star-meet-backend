🌟 StarMeet Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Overview

StarMeet provides AI virtual character chat services. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, nickname, age, gender, region, avatar, and account identifier.
  • Chat Content: Messages you send to AI characters, including text, images, and voice messages.
  • Usage Data: App settings, preferences, and interaction history.
  • Purchase Records: Subscription and in-app purchase history via Apple App Store.
  • Security and Service Data: Login IP, preferences, search and interaction history, and an APNs device token when notifications are enabled.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve AI character chat functionality.
  • To manage your account and subscription status.
  • To enable features like memory system and character recommendations.
  • To ensure content safety and compliance.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We use the following third-party services:

  • Apple StoreKit: For in-app purchases and subscription verification.
  • AI Model Providers: Chat prompts and relevant conversation context are processed to generate AI character responses.
  • Cloud Storage: For storing media files.

Data Retention

Your data is retained while your account is active and only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can permanently delete your account in the App settings. Legal, fraud-prevention, and payment records may be retained where required by law.

Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Cancel subscriptions at any time via App Store settings.
  • Disable personalized features in app settings.

Children's Privacy

StarMeet is intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly allow minors to create or use an account. If we learn that a minor provided personal information, we will restrict the account and delete the information unless retention is legally required.

意見回饋與使用事件 / Feedback and product events

你主動提交的回饋正文、選填聯絡方式及版本資訊用於處理問題。產品事件包括隨機安裝標識、操作步驟、回覆耗時、錯誤碼和帳號關聯，用於分析首聊與回訪；事件不包含聊天正文、密碼、廣告識別碼或推送令牌，不用於跨 App 追蹤。

Feedback contains your submitted text, optional contact details and app/device information. First-party product events use a random installation ID, bounded action metadata, timings, error codes and an account link after sign-in. Events do not include chat text, passwords, advertising identifiers or push tokens and are not used for cross-app tracking.
